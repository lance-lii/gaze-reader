Let Gaze Reader see where you’re reading

Gaze Reader watches your eyes through the webcam and turns the page when you reach the bottom, so you never have to reach for the scroll wheel. Chrome needs your OK before it can use the camera.

  • Stays on this device. Video is processed right here and is never recorded, uploaded or stored. The only thing downloaded is the face-tracking model.
  • Only while you use it. The camera runs only while Gaze Reader is on in a tab you are looking at, and the page shows a camera icon while it does.
  • Easy to switch off. Click × on the page, press Alt+Shift+G, or read with the mouse instead.
